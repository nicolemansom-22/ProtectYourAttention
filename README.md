# Protect Your Attention
Through a shooting-style game, Protect Your Attention aims to raise awareness about the importance of being intentional with where you direct your focus by eliminating distractions and prioritising what truly matters.


## General Information
A shooting-style game that raises awareness about the negative effects of doomscrolling. Players must defend themselves against distractions, encouraging them to think about how excessive scrolling can impact their attention. The game aims to address the issue of doomscrolling, where people spend long periods scrolling through negative or endless content online. Doomscrolling can reduce attention spans, decrease productivity, and negatively affect mental health. This is why Protect Your Attention educates players about the importance of managing their attention in a digital environment. By turning the concept into an interactive shooting game, players learn about the challenges of staying focused while developing an understanding of how social media and constant online content can distract them
